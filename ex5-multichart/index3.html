TV energy and electricity prices

Three views of the cost of running a television in Australia: how much energy different screen technologies use, how wholesale electricity prices have moved, and which screen sizes dominate the market.

Energy use by screen technology

Among 55-inch TVs, LED models use the most energy on average and LCD models the least.

Average electricity spot price, 1998–2024

The average spot price across the four mainland states has more than tripled since 1998, with the sharpest rise after 2020.

TV models by screen size

Just over half of the models on sale are medium sized (43–65 inches), and large screens outnumber small ones almost two to one.

Sources: Australian Government Energy Rating Registry – Televisions, downloaded 15 Feb 2026 (filtered to models available in Australia); electricity spot prices (ARE_Spot_Prices.csv) supplied with the unit.

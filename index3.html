Demonstration 2

Four exercise pages built with SVG and D3 v7, using television data from the Australian Government Energy Rating Registry.

COS30045 Data Visualisation, Week 7.

Exercises

  • 4.1

    SVG house

    A house drawn from basic SVG shapes, with a toggle that shows the coordinates of each shape type.

    Open Exercise 4.1

  • 4.2–4.6

    Bar chart of TV brands

    A horizontal bar chart of TV models registered per brand, loaded from CSV with D3 scales. Exercise 4.2 has its own D3 basics page.

    Open the bar chart Open D3 basics (4.2)

  • 5.1–5.3

    Multi-chart page

    One page with a vertical bar chart of energy use by screen technology, a line chart of electricity spot prices and a donut chart of screen sizes.

    Open Exercise 5

  • 6.1–6.4

    Interactivity

    A histogram with screen technology and size filters, a star-rating scatterplot and hover tooltips.

    Open Exercise 6
